Privacy Policy

Parsec Chronicles 3D (Android app and web version) · Last updated: 25 November 2026

The short version

Parsec Chronicles 3D does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the game stores, and where

To remember your progress the game saves the following only on your own device, in the app's local storage:

  • Pilot profiles you create (the pilot name you type, campaign progress, credits, upgrades, cosmetics and Deeds)
  • Settings (graphics, sound, controls, key bindings and similar options)

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it and cannot get it back for you.

Network access

The game runs fully offline. It does not send data over the internet. On the web version, your browser may download the game files from our website host (GitHub Pages), which, like any web server, may log basic technical information such as your IP address. See GitHub's privacy statement for details. The Android app itself does not contact any server.

Permissions

The Android app does not ask for any dangerous permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts or files). It may vibrate the phone for haptic feedback if you leave haptics switched on.

Copying a pilot

If you use Export Pilot, the game shows your save as text and can copy it to your clipboard. Nothing is sent anywhere; what you do with that text is up to you.

Deleting your data

Delete a pilot from the Pilots screen in the game, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Parsec Chronicles 3D > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example by adding cloud saves), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please use the developer contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
